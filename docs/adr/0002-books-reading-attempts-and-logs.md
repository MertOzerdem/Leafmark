# Separate book pages from reading activity

Keep one private book page per title, with editable catalog details and reusable or book-specific fields; store each completion as a separate reading attempt with its own dates and rating, and store dated daily totals as reading logs linked to a book. This keeps rereads on the same book page, lets reading activity remain independent from book notes, and moves a finished book from TBR into the read-books library and visual bookshelf.
